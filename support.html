Support

Need help with Packing Pilot? Review these common questions or contact us.

Where are my trips saved?

Trips and packing lists are saved locally on the device where you created them. The current version does not offer account-based syncing between devices.

How can I reuse a packing list?

Use the app's duplicate-trip option to make a new copy. Packed-item checkmarks are reset in the duplicate.

Will reinstalling restore my trips?

Not necessarily. The current version does not provide an in-app backup or recovery feature. Avoid deleting the app if you need to keep your trips.
