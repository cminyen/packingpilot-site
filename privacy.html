Packing Pilot

Privacy Policy

Effective: 18 October 2026

Packing Pilot is a travel packing-list app. This policy describes how the current version handles information.

Information you enter

Trip names, destinations, dates, trip types, packing items and checklist progress are stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account.

Data collection and sharing

The current app does not send your trip information to the developer or operate its own servers. It does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and the developer does not sell or share your trip information.

Storage and deletion

Your trip data is saved on your device. You can remove individual trips and packing items within the app. Removing the app may delete locally stored trip data. Device backups may be handled by Apple according to your device settings.

Children

The app is a general-purpose packing-list tool and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If the app's data-handling practices change, this policy will be updated before or alongside the relevant release.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method on our support page.

This policy describes the reviewed Packing Pilot v0.2 source. Verify it against the final release build before publication.
